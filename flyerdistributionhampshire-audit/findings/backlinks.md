## Key Point: Fix Schema Before Building Citations

The LocalBusiness schema has no `@id` property. When Google encounters a new citation for "Flyer Distribution Hampshire" on Yell.com, it cannot reliably associate it with this specific URL entity. Add the `@id` first; then build citations that deliver full value.

## Priority 1 — Week 1 (Critical)

**A. Google Business Profile**
The highest-ROI first action. The GBP listing generates a maps.google.com URL that becomes the cornerstone `sameAs` entry. Without GBP, no other link-building reaches its full potential. Setup: business.google.com → Service Area Business → enter real address (hidden from public) → set service area Hampshire + Dorset → verify by postcard or video call → add categories, services, photos.

**B. Fix `@id` and `sameAs` in LocalBusiness schema**
20 minutes. Every citation submitted before this is slightly less effective.

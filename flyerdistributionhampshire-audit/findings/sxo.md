## User Stories Identified (from PAA + related searches)

1. **The Sceptic** (score 38/100): "I've been let down before — how do I know my leaflets actually get delivered?" Needs: GPS tracking detail, completion reports, specific mechanism named.

2. **The Budget Researcher** (score 42/100): "What will this cost me before I fill in a form?" Needs: indicative pricing, even a range.

3. **The First-Timer** (score 48/100): "I've never done this before — how does it actually work?" Needs: How It Works section, educational blog content.

4. **The City-Specific Searcher** (score 51/100): Clicks city page, needs to see local context + trust signals. Currently gets a thin template page.

5. **The Repeat Buyer** (score 58/100): Knows the product, comparing on reliability and price. Strongest persona fit — the quote form signals operational seriousness.

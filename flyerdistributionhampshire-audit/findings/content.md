## Critical E-E-A-T Gaps

**C1. Business is entirely anonymous**
No named founder, director, or team member anywhere on the site. The About page has three "values" cards but no individual. This is the single highest-impact Trust gap under September 2025 QRG — local service businesses with named, verifiable individuals significantly outperform anonymous operators.

**C2. Zero social proof**
No customer testimonials, no review count, no star rating, no named client examples, no case studies. Competitors surface 4+ star ratings and named testimonials above the fold.

**C3. No phone number**
Complete absence across all pages including the About and Quote pages. A primary trust signal for any local service.

## High Priority

**H1. Services page: 410 words vs 800+ needed**
Add: How It Works (3–4 steps), per-service "who it suits" copy, turnaround expectations, minimum quantities. Service descriptions on this page are nearly identical to homepage cards — low unique value.

**H2. About page: 270 words, no identifying information**
Add: founding date, owner name and short bio, town-level location ("based in [town], Hampshire"), team background.

**H3. All area pages: 200–270 words vs 500+ needed**
Each needs one area-specific content element not found elsewhere: demographic statistic, local landmark, specific business-use case, or geography note. New Forest and Southampton are the strongest models.

**H4. Areas hub: 220 words**
Add 2–3 paragraphs of geographic narrative. "Also Covering" tag cloud should become a short descriptive paragraph with context.

## Medium Priority

**M1. BH12 postcode overlap**
BH12 appears on both Bournemouth and Poole pages. Geographically accurate but should be acknowledged to avoid confusion.

**M2. FAQ missing 3 high-intent questions**
"How much does leaflet distribution cost in Hampshire?", "How do I know my leaflets were delivered?", "Are my leaflets distributed alone or shared?" — the top PAA queries for this vertical are all unanswered.

**M3. Internal linking weak outside /quote**
Every page links to /quote (good). But: /services doesn't link to /areas; area pages don't cross-link; /faq and /about have no links to /areas or /services. FAQ text answers "what areas do you cover?" but doesn't hyperlink to area pages.

**M4. Meta titles on 4 pages are generic or missing geo**
- /services: "Leaflet Distribution Services" — missing Hampshire & Dorset
- /faq: "FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions" — redundant + no geo
- /about: "About Us" — generic
- /quote: "Get a Free Quote" — generic

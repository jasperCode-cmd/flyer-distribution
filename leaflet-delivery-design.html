Leaflet delivery questions

More answers on our FAQ page.

How do I know my leaflets were delivered?

Every round is GPS tracked, and when your campaign is complete we send you a route report showing the areas we covered.

Can I choose exactly where my leaflets go?

Yes. Tell us the postcodes or areas you want and we'll plan the rounds around them. Not sure? We'll suggest the areas most likely to respond.

Who delivers my leaflets?

Our own local team across Hampshire and Dorset.

Can you print them as well?

Yes. Choose Print and Deliver, or Design, Print and Deliver if you need the artwork too. See leaflet printing.

Do you deliver outside Hampshire and Dorset?

We deliver from Winchester to Poole and everywhere in between. If you're just outside, ask us and we'll let you know.
